Q1 Where from?
Source region in eight broad classes — local, east, south, west, north, Saharan, polar, mixed.
via HYSPLIT trajectory
Q2 How will it evolve?
Concentration trajectory at +24, +48, +72 h with trend arrow and projected peak time.
via CAMS European ENSEMBLE
Q3 What type?
Dust, biomass-burning smoke, urban/industrial, secondary anthropogenic, pollen, or mixed.
via CAMS species + pollen
Q4 Where vertically?
Ground-level, boundary-layer-mixed, or elevated-only — three-level descriptor.
via Ceilometer + AOD/PM ratio
Q5 Act or monitor?
No action, monitor, prepare advisory, public advisory, or emergency action — source-aware escalation.
via integrated logic
Q6 Scale of danger?
EU AQI five-level categorisation — good, fair, moderate, poor, very poor.
via RNMCA + CAMS fusion
