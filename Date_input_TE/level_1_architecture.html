Q1 Where from?
Quantitative source attribution by region and sector, chemistry-confirmed by upwind TROPOMI NO₂/SO₂/CO hotspots.
via FLEXPART × emissions + TROPOMI
Q2 How will it evolve?
Regional CAMS forecast, upstream CAMS Global heads-up, local FLEXPART plume forecast, plus live SEVIRI/FCI observation.
via CAMS × 2 + FLEXPART + geo-sat
Q3 What type?
Per-layer microphysics with chemistry verification — mode, SSA, refractive index, gas fingerprints, detailed chemistry on case-study runs.
via GRASP + TROPOMI + GEOS-Chem
Q4 Where vertically?
Full vertical profile with depolarization-based dust/smoke separation and HSRL-derived extinction for upwind regions.
via GRASP + ceilo + EARLINET + ATLID
Q5 Act or monitor?
Source-quantitative, chemistry-aware, live-observation-confirmed action with extended health protocols for heavy metals and POPs.
via integrated logic + EMEP
Q6 Scale of danger?
Multi-source validated EU AQI plus chemistry-aware health risk for specific hazards (waste fires, industrial accidents).
via RNMCA + AERONET + CAMS + EMEP
